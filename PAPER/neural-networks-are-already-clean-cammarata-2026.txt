@@BEGIN:CASE_SUMMARY_ZH@@
Nick Cammarata（Goodfire）的《Neural Networks Are Already Clean》提出神經元及其迴路遠比既有多義性／疊加敘事更容易理解的工作假說。本次來源型審查認為：原生神經元軸的特殊性及部分局部迴路的可解釋性具有正面證據；但大部分神經元僅一項主要功能、弱活化不重要、全模型機制已普遍乾淨等強主張，仍欠穩健陰性控制、跨活化區間預測與廣泛因果驗證。按一般實證研究投稿標準，審稿建議 MAJOR REVISION，非期刊正式決定。
@@END:CASE_SUMMARY_ZH@@
@@BEGIN:LIHUO_REVIEW_ZH@@
【Q0｜主張邊界】
此處 clean 意指神經元／迴路可讀性，不是無偏見、已對齊或安全完成驗證。
C1 經典多義性案例可能曾被誤讀：局部重檢值得支持。
C2 神經元軸相較隨機方向在部分測量下有特殊結構：部分支持。
C3 約九成 Qwen3-32B 神經元極性一項工作：初步估計，對定義和方法敏感。
C4 弱活化普遍不重要：跨模型與干預設定不穩健。
C5 recursive generalization 全面取代 superposition：未充分實證。
C6 大型網路機制普遍已理解或 AI 安全已被證明：超出證據。
語義標籤可讀性，不等於完整活化預測，更不等於下游因果效應預測。

REVIEW_EN_PROVENANCE: TRANSLATION_OF_SAME_LIHUO_REVIEW
@@BEGIN:CASE_SUMMARY_EN@@
Nick Cammarata's Goodfire essay "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" presents a working hypothesis that native neurons and circuits are far more legible than previous polysemanticity/superposition narratives suggested. The available evidence supports meaningful neuron-axis structure and some locally interpretable circuits, but does not yet establish that most neurons have one reliably identifiable function, that weak activations are broadly irrelevant, or that large models are generally clean in the causal sense. Recommendation if assessed as an empirical research paper: MAJOR REVISION, not an editorial decision by any journal.
@@END:CASE_SUMMARY_EN@@
@@BEGIN:LIHUO_REVIEW_EN@@
【Scope and claims】
Here, clean concerns neuron/circuit legibility, not bias elimination, alignment or safety proof. C1 localized reinterpretation of classic polysemanticity deserves investigation; C2 special neuron-axis structure is partially supported; C3 roughly 90% one-job Qwen3-32B neuron poles is a method-sensitive preliminary estimate; C4 weak activations broadly irrelevant is not robust; C5 recursive generalization displaces superposition as a general mechanism is not established; C6 most large models fully understood or safe is beyond current evidence.
Readable labels are not necessarily accurate whole-distribution activation predictions or causal downstream effect predictions.

【Source comparison】
S1 Cammarata (2026-10-15): visual/language neuron examples, local causal circuits, preliminary one-job estimate (https://already-clean.goodfire.com/).
S3 leogao (2026-10-17): critique of label-based functional inference (https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Dkh7pXKjoNWkkxQDz/neural-networks-are-not-already-clean).
S4 IRSI (2026-10-18): approx 78.3% one-job under its operationalization of Goodfire texts, but positive evidence for native neuron axes and activation-prediction tasks; not peer reviewed (https://irsi.ai/notes/already-clean/).
S5 Batutin (2026-10-19): small-Qwen negative controls found one-job tags on 84% random-init vs 52% trained neurons and 0/50 label-only downstream predictions above matched controls; not a direct replication of the original 32B (https://andriybatutin.substack.com/p/we-tried-to-confirm-neural-networks).

【Visible reference-level LIHUO cycle】
R1-REF: A broad clean-neuron optimism provisionally led an author-evidence-first reconstruction against B calibrated local interpretability and C remaining superposition/distributed mechanisms. R1 was reconstructed after seeing critiques, so it was not a separately sealed first round.
O9-REF: identify high-activation selection, negative-control gaps, label-to-causal inference, and local-to-global extrapolation. Textual observation, not actual Main O9 telemetry.
I9: no verified authorized trigger, intervention or machine receipt.
R2-REF: recheck alternatives using independent challenges; B becomes the provisional evidence-backed leader.
R3-REF: the R2 reversal prompted a re-challenge using strongest author circuit evidence and positive independent neuron-axis tests; B persists as the best bounded statement. No formally qualified two-round victory is claimed.
Termination: this scoped public-source review may conclude, but the empirical mechanism debate remains open. No formal Main termination or Lighter release.

【Required revision】
Define operational labels/thresholds/sampling and inter-rater reliability; add random-init, random-axis and label-swap negative controls; test held-out activations across the activation distribution; pre-register label-derived predictions of downstream ablation/amplification effects; distinguish recursive generalization from superposition experimentally; replicate across sizes/architectures; release failures and methods; constrain all-model and safety extrapolation.

【Decision】
MAJOR REVISION as a reference-level editorial recommendation, not peer-review acceptance/rejection, and not a formal LIHUO Final Gate decision. The linked companion report preserves details and unverified execution stages: PAPER/neural-networks-are-already-clean-cammarata-2026-lihuo-cycle-report.md
