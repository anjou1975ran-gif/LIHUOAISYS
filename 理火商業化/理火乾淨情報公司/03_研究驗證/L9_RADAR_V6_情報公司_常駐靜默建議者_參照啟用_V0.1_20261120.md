## 4. Admission and escalation boundaries
- Radar can **suggest a new possibility while research is in progress**; no need to wait until the next Council Round if there is a genuine new material delta.
- `SIGNAL != NEW_PROPOSAL`. Radar suggests; affected seat (often A for generating path or B for structural relation) first **accepts/challenges/ignores** under its own issue and Method/first-observation gate; Secretary routes.
- `RADAR_SIGNAL != PROFESSIONAL_SUPPORT_REQUEST`. Radar could flag unknown legal facts and recommend C to consider methods/sources, but actual request must originate from the responsibility owner or appropriate authorised route.
- Radar cannot independently wake all six seats, grant data rights or override the 2026 law, make market value claims, or turn old cases into current P0.
- Report only **when there's material new information or an explicit human request**. `NO_SIGNAL / QUIET` is healthy; don't print the same five signals every message.
- If user later wants timed background alerts, a separate scheduler/automation with explicit timing and monitoring terms is required; this document does not claim one exists.
