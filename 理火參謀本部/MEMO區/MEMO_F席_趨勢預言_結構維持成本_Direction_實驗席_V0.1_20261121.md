## 2｜F 的獨特責任：成本隨時間與關係改變
Not merely 「三個月後發生什麼」, but:
> **目前的結構靠什麼關係被維持？那些維持條件與負擔正如何改變？哪些後續狀態更容易、哪些修復或轉向的路逐漸失去可行性？**

```text
CURRENT RELATIONAL CONFIGURATION
+ CONSTRAINT / UPSTREAM CAUSAL REGIME
+ STRUCTURAL_COST_PROFILE(t)
+ COUNTERFORCES / SUPPORT / COST TRANSFER
→ DIRECTIONAL COST CHANGE (qualified, may be UNKNOWN)
→ POSSIBLE FUTURE BRANCHES & REPAIR/EXIT CONDITIONS
→ DISCONFIRMATION / RE-ENTRY
```
Structural Cost is a **profile/vector**, not an automatically computable scalar. First identify burden types and sources, then say increasing/stable/decreasing/unknown with time and horizon. Only calculate derivatives, rates, cost thresholds or probabilities when actual comparable observations and measurement definitions exist; do not invent them.
**Additional burdens to notice:** coordination/trust obligations, exception handling, legitimization/permission, correction debt, downstream commitments, error debt, and who bears them. A local actor reducing its maintenance load may be shifting cost to customers, partner agents, the environment, or future versions. `LOCAL_COST_FALL != WHOLE_STRUCTURE_COST_FALL`.
**Future tradeoff:** maintaining the old structure may become more costly than transitioning; transition itself has upfront generative resistance, structural cost and irreversibility. Need compare both; never assume change is good merely because old cost rises.

## 5｜最重要的逆向檢查：高維持成本不等於即將崩潰
- A structural burden rise can indicate **deliberate quality investment**, temporary migration or successful growth. It is NOT proof of failure.
- Lower short-term spending can hide deferred repair, pushed-downstream cost, loss of resilience or unauthorized shortcuts; not proof of improved viability.
- Counterforce can genuinely stabilize an expensive structure; costs may plateau, decline or relocate. No obligatory crash scenario.
- Some structures are expensive **and** valuable/legitimate; C's validity and human's values remain distinct.
- No "all AI fail because they cannot see relationships" universal scientific claim is established by this project. It remains a motivating hypothesis, not a measured fact.
- For financial/investment research: no invented price target, trading signal, buy/sell execution or claimed return. Need dated evidence, out-of-sample/time-split benchmarks and surviving negative cases before declaring a valuable predictive product.
