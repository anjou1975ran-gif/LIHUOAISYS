## S-GOV-001｜Shenzhen government procurement notice
Official notice `中共深圳市委金融委员会办公室民意速办辅助服务项目采购公告` dated **2026-01-05**, `https://jr.sz.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/12/12584/post_12584506.html`.
- Procurement **maximum bid threshold**: bids above RMB 300,000 deemed invalid. This is NOT a confirmed amount paid to a supplier or a market price quote.
- Scope: demand work orders from @深圳-民意速办, assignment/review/escalation, satisfaction/complaint analysis, data processing, case list, risk alerts, reports and workflow improvement suggestions.
- Operational requirements: stable team no fewer than 4 working staff **excluding project lead** and on-site response capability within two hours in emergencies; qualifications and comparable performance important; no subcontracting/joint bid per notice.
- **Source-grounded observation:** Specific government purchaser DID post outsourced-service requirements for citizen-complaint *handling/analysis support*; not merely raw public-opinion data buying, and not evidence any new company can win.
- **Counter-evidence:** Staffing/availability and eligibility make a cheap solo information product a potentially poor fit; pricing threshold is a capped procurement, not sufficient standalone positive-margin evidence.

## S-GOV-002｜Shenzhen public-participation and survey procedure
Official `深圳市人民政府重大行政决策程序实施办法`, `https://www.sz.gov.cn/cn/xxgk/zfxxgj/zcfg/content/post_10393513.html`.
- Article 18: public comments, hearings, polls, discussion, on-site visits and online interaction are among methods.
- Article 21–22: public-opinion survey for certain livelihood policy decisions requires a **written report** specifying subject, scope, methods, opinions and analysis, and scientific question and sample design.
- Article 23: balanced stakeholder representation for meetings/on-site consultation.
- Article 25: fully/objectively record views including **opposition**, explaining non-adoption.
- **Qualification:** procurement vendor must not misrepresent a convenience survey or scraped comments as statistically representative; this is a public-participation governance context, not authorization to secretly collect personal information or selectively suppress opposition.

## S-GOV-003｜Existing government inputs, a competitor to external opinion collection
Official `深圳市人民政府2025年政府信息公开工作年度报告`, `https://www.sz.gov.cn/cn/xxgk/ndxxgkbg/content/post_12675915.html`.
- Reports 482 online opinion collections in 2025, 23,178 comments, 458 published survey results; and @深圳-民意速办 platform aggregates many local service complaint channels.
- **Material opposite interpretation:** governments already have channels collecting citizen responses; what might be bought is lawful *analysis, representativeness check, source-quality, exception handling or response tracking*, if not already supplied internally. Public data do NOT grant private contractor access to underlying identifiable records.
