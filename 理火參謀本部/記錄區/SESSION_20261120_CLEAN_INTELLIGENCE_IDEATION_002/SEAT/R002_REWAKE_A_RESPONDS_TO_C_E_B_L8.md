## R1 material competition
- **Old seductive idea:** let source contributors recruit sub-contributors and take a percentage of all subsequent information sales; could grow fast.
  **Objection consumed:** real customer pays for verified data, not recruits; this creates PRC pyramid risk and diverts compensation from verified work.
- **Alternative A2-REF:** existing contributors share invitations or a task link; no pay-to-join, no downline structure or multi-layer recruitment commission; if any referral incentive, direct, transparent and legally reviewed, not a condition of source grade.
- **Alternative A2-RELAY:** a requester commissions observation; originator, independent verifier, and analyst each get a fee for **their own accepted contribution**, not for recruiting the next actor.
- **Alternative A2-GRADE:** a good source may access more demanding assignments, but its past successes cannot turn an unverified observation into guaranteed truth.
- **Alternative A2-EXCLUSIVE:** limited-time access to commercially useful, authorized information; buyer-specific price may be high even if source rank is moderate, but rights and confidentiality gate must pass.

## R2 consumes T1 — Owned substantive position revision
**Material stance change:** A withdraws any interpretation of A-09/A-10 that lets a higher-ranking source profit from a recruited downline. Instead:
1. **Reward role is separate from source rank.** Only named, accepted tasks and permitted licensed reuse can pay contributors, verifiers, or originators; no proxy for recruitment.
2. **Viral refers to distribution of task demand, not profit from multiplying members.** A shareable job card might spread because people who can legally verify the requested fact want the job.
3. **New A-16｜支付只在成交與驗證後發生**: buyer's accepted request determines scope; contributors paid for useful output. Escrow/payment settlement would need appropriate legal/payment provider review, not automatic funds custody.
4. **New A-17｜驗證者反而是主收入**: questionable but lawfully obtained lead sold only as a *verification service request*, not sold as established fact.
5. **Retain A-14 cooperation network** as DORMANT_RIGHTS_UNPROVEN; not banned forever.
6. **Source tier controls safe task eligibility; buyer willingness + legal license control price.** This directly revises "high-tier source sells high-price data" assumption rather than merely adding a caveat.

## R3 reconnection
A now groups its 17 source ideas into **five generating mechanisms**, no winning product:
1. **REQUESTED INTELLIGENCE:** bounty task, relay verification, buyer group procurement of research.
2. **VOLUNTARY SOURCE FEEDS:** retailers/suppliers publish licensed stock/promos/capacity, opt-in cooperation pool.
3. **URBAN MICRO-OBSERVATION:** only authorized/low-risk observations with sampling, mobile submission and QA; mass submissions can still be worthless.
4. **NEGATIVE / CORRECTION INFORMATION:** no-anomaly attestations, quality challenges, expiration, correction and revoke alerts.
5. **DELIVERY / DISTRIBUTION PRODUCTS:** legal priority windows, buyer-specific/packaged signals, source/API packages, shareable task cards.

Every path carries a buyer, evidence, R-use and C/E cost. A does NOT select "app/social levels" as obligatory company identity.
