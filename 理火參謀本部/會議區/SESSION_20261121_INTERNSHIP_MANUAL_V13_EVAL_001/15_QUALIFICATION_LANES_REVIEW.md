## Proposed P1 candidate amendment (not applied)
If keeping four *human-visible* lanes, rename D tentatively to `VERTICAL_SPECIFIC_COMPETENCE_AND_AUTHORIZATION` (name itself under revision). Internally, two separable non-transitive fields:
- **D1 DOMAIN_OR_ROLE_COMPETENCE_EVIDENCE:** what role/domain task the actor demonstrably knows how to do, with scoped evidence assessor. `COMPETENT != PERMITTED`.
- **D2 ROLE_AUTHORIZATION_DECISION:** actual authority grant by authorized human/Vertical/world owner; `PERMITTED != COMPETENT`.
Never write `D_PASS` as a single summary. When one side is untested, show `NOT_ASSESSED / UNKNOWN`; when permission denied, show `NOT_GRANTED`. If five top-level lanes prove necessary for legitimate native evidence, that option survives as a future candidate, but it is **not required as a universal default** now.

1. Skilled tool operator with no owner consent: D1 supported, D2 NOT_GRANTED; no execution.

3. Guanzhi narrator preserves player and NPC autonomy without a Staff-HQ L8 output: B capability may be supported by world-native evidence; C full Council L8 skill is NOT_ASSESSED; world/role grants remain the actual owner question.
