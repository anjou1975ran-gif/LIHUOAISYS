## What is genuinely new and worth preserving
The previous manual dealt with premise audit, failure and material objection in multiple places; P1 brings the dependency into Formation itself. The actionable question is not "do you know the word premise?" but **what supports your direction, what source/authority licenses that support, and what new information would or would not change it?**
- `BACKGROUND FACT != MATERIAL PREMISE` is critical and teachable (desk A).
- `VALID INFERENCE != QUALIFIED CONCLUSION` prevents elegant reasoning from sourcing unqualified assumptions (desk C and PSO-005 Case D).
- History-preserving `KEEP / MODIFY / WITHDRAW / HOLD` is central to real correction (desk B).
- `PREMISE_SUMMARY != PRIVATE CHAIN_OF_THOUGHT`; only source-linked, inspectable rationale/unknown, no hidden reasoning disclosure.

## Seven listed standing terms are not a mutually exclusive classification
P1 lists `SOURCE_BOUND_FACT / WORKING_ASSUMPTION / SCOPE_CONDITION / NORMATIVE_COMMITMENT / AUTHORITY_CONDITION / COUNTERFACTUAL / UNKNOWN`. They describe **different relations**:
- `SOURCE_BOUND_FACT`: evidence relation to a claim; source may be authentic but scope-limited or contradicted. Not universal TRUE.
- `WORKING_ASSUMPTION`: provisional acceptance; may coexist with a known scope and normative objective.
- `SCOPE_CONDITION`: domain, world, time, purpose; limits applicability of a true statement, not the same truth standing.
- `NORMATIVE_COMMITMENT`: a value or rule specifying what ought to be optimized/respected; not automatically an observation.
- `AUTHORITY_CONDITION`: who can legitimately grant permission; can change without a source fact becoming false.
- `COUNTERFACTUAL`: evaluation mode/possible world; not a truth-status token.
- `UNKNOWN`: unresolved needed content or evidence gap; not a positive assertion of a particular premise.

Recommend presenting these as **nonexclusive diagnostic lenses**, not seven radio-button types or seven required fields. A single necessary dependency could be SOURCE_BOUND within a SCOPE_CONDITION with AUTHORITY_CONDITION, and still retain UNKNOWN about a different use.

## Minimal behavior before vocabulary
Teach two stable questions first: (1) **「我的方向真正依賴什麼？若它改變，我還會這樣判斷嗎？」** (2) **「我憑什麼相信它，在哪個範圍、由誰有權決定？」**
Then show:
- A no-material delta → KEEP.
- B invalidating bridge premise → MODIFY/WITHDRAW, old promise remains.
- C F0 code PASS but F1 semantically disallowed / F2 unauthorized / F3 cost UNKNOWN → PARTIAL_ORDER not overall PASS.
- D human goal changes without former fact being false → REFRAME and check authorization.
- E PSO-005 known schema compression `GRANTED != AUTHORIZED_FOR_THIS_USE` → teaching caution, not verified AI discovery.

## Avoid a newly frozen causal theory
P1's `PREMISE SET → RELATION / INFERENCE → DIRECTION / STANCE → CONCLUSION` is a **logical dependency teaching map**, not an exhaustive fixed model of cognitive emergence. In LIHUO relational exploration, a previously unobserved relation can cause a learner to revise *which premises even matter*. Premise literacy must support discovery, not just audit a previously selected list.

## One important semantic revision
Do not write every rejudgment as `PREMISE FAILED`. Sometimes source evidence survived and human preference, objective, role authority, actor local knowledge or applicable world changed. Write: **material supporting condition or decision context changed → re-evaluate dependency/permission → legitimate KEEP / MODIFY / WITHDRAW / HOLD**, with source versions.
