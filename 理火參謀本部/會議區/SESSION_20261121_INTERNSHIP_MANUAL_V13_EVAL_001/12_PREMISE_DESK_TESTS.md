## Case A — IRRELEVANT BACKGROUND / KEEP

- Candidate material premise: opening hours allow the planned visit; font color is merely background under this goal.

- Scope: if the user's task had been to compare font legibility or signage branding, color could be material. Materiality is goal-relative, not an object's eternal status.
## Case B — SINGLE MATERIAL DEPENDENCY FAILS / REVISE WITHOUT RETCON
