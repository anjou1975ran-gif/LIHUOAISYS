## External research source packet
These are external source returns, not Council stances:
1. Luo et al. (2026), From Storage to Experience: A Survey on the Evolution of LLM Agent Memory Mechanisms, Findings of ACL 2026. https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.2069/ — proposes Storage / Reflection / Experience abstraction; survey framework, not proof of autonomous evolution.
2. Wang et al. (2025), How Far Can LLMs Improve from Experience? Measuring Test-Time Learning Ability in LLMs with Human Comparison, EMNLP 2025. https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1304/ — reports measurable test-time learning but unstable cumulative gains versus human participants.
3. Xiong et al. (2025), How Memory Management Impacts LLM Agents: An Empirical Study of Experience-Following Behavior. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16067 — identifies error propagation and misaligned experience replay; selective addition/deletion improved results in its controlled tasks.
4. Kumar et al. (2025), Training Language Models to Self-Correct via Reinforcement Learning, ICLR 2025. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/871ac99fdc5282d0301934d23945ebaa-Abstract-Conference.html — trained self-correction through multi-turn online RL; not evidence that a normal inference-time self-critique loop alone is durable learning.
5. Belikova et al. (2026), Managing Procedural Memory in LLM Agents: Control, Adaptation, and Evaluation (AFTER). https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23127 — 382 tasks, six roles, 22 skills; shows both improvement and cross-role transfer limitations.
6. Zhao et al. (2026), Self-Evolving Agents Can Repair Failures but Struggle to Generalize from Them. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22984 — reports a repair/generalization gap in failure-only evolution settings.
