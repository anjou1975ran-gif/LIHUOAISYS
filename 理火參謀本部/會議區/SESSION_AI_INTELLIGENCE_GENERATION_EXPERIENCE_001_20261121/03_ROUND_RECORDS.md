## R2｜Concept and Premise Audit
**Task:** Separate intelligence, LLM, AI system, experience, method, innovation, invention and self-evolution.
- Intelligence is operationalized for this study as adaptive goal-directed capability under constraints, including transfer; this is a working definition, not a universal philosophical settlement.
- LLM is a model component; an AI system may additionally include agent control, memory, tools, environment, verifiers, data, people and governance.
- Pretraining, post-training/RL, in-context adaptation, external memory, procedural skill, method abstraction, weight updates and architecture evolution are separate mechanisms.
- Experience is not the transcript itself; it needs an event/context, action, outcome, feedback, provenance and an assessed interpretation.
- A method changes the first observation, evidence selection, causal framing, scope and blind spots; it is not merely a fact, prompt or task recipe.
**Challenge:** A polished self-explanation does not prove a learning event; a repeated answer can be retrieval, imitation or overfitting.
**New events:** EV-0012…EV-0013.
**Unresolved:** No single universal definition of intelligence; no direct observation of model internals.

## R3｜Innovation Generation Mechanism
**Task:** Use the rice-ball analogy, then test its transfer to AI.
- Existing elements: rice, filling, wrapper, process.
- Constraints: health, taste, cost, preservation, production and market.
- Variation: ingredient, grain, wrapping, frying/baking/steaming and workflow.
- Interaction: preparation and use reveal actual performance.
- Feedback/selection: taste, safety, cost, shelf-life and repeatability determine which structure stabilizes.
- AI analogy: base models, method packs, tools, memory, environment, evaluators and interaction protocols are the elements; a new capability requires a new relation or organized process plus evidence of improvement.
**Demand-driven vs open exploration:** both can share variation→interaction→feedback→selection→retention, but differ in entry objective and selection criterion. Open exploration is not unbounded; it remains limited by resources, environment and governance.
**New events:** EV-0014…EV-0015.
**Unresolved:** How to reward exploration without collapsing diversity; how to distinguish new relation from re-description.

## R4｜Method and Experience Floor
**Task:** Determine whether a method can serve as an AI cognitive seed.
- Knowledge provides content.
- Prompt provides temporary task framing.
- Skill provides a task procedure.
- Agent distillation compresses trajectories or behavior patterns; it may preserve local workarounds without generalizable observation.
- Method provides an observation contract: first question, analysis unit, evidence preference, causal model, blind spots, use/suppression conditions and transfer limits.
**Finding:** Method Packs can serve as a reusable cognitive floor or prior for exploration, but do not themselves constitute intelligence or guarantee correct judgment. Their value must be tested by method-shift and held-out transfer tests; they cannot confer truth or specialist authority.
**New events:** EV-0016…EV-0017.
**Unresolved:** Comparative tests against prompts, skills and distillation; method distinctness and bloat control.

## R5｜Experience Accumulation and Exchange
**Task:** Build an experience lifecycle and exchange architecture.
- E0 raw episode/trajectory (immutable source).
- E1 outcome and feedback record (what happened).
- E2 verified diagnosis/reflection (why, with counterexamples and uncertainty).
- E3 procedural skill (how to perform a recurring task).
- E4 cross-trajectory abstraction / method candidate (what general relation or observation shift recurs).
- E5 validated method or policy update (requires held-out transfer and regression tests).
- E6 parameter/architecture change (requires separate training and release governance).
**Exchange:** package source/version, task scope, actions/outcomes, verifier, attribution, method refs, transfer conditions, failure cases, cost, privacy/IP, expiry and rollback. Receiver must perform compatibility and transfer checks; sharing is not acceptance.
**Counterevidence:** memory errors propagate; outdated or misaligned replay can harm future performance.
**New events:** EV-0018…EV-0020.
**Unresolved:** Interoperability, privacy/IP, conflict resolution, revalidation and retirement.

## R6｜B × E Adversarial Cost Round
**B architecture 1 — Capability Ecology:** a population of models, tools and methods evolves at system level through task interaction, exchange, selection and recombination.
**B architecture 2 — External Cognitive Substrate / Method Compiler:** experience graph stores qualified episodes, skills and methods; compiler composes a task-specific cognitive runtime; model backbone can be replaced.
**E response:** neither design eliminates cost. Capability Ecology moves cost into model orchestration, communication, selection, security and integration. Method Compiler moves cost into abstraction validation, version control, transfer tests, memory maintenance and human review.
**C response:** only held-out evidence can distinguish transferable capability from local repair. Benchmark gaming, false failure attribution and poisoned memory remain major risks.
**N1 response:** judge by user-visible reduction in repeated instructions/errors and better unseen-task performance, not internal memory volume or new terminology.
**New discovery:** cost relocation, not cost disappearance. The relevant economic unit should be cost per validated transferable capability increment.
**New events:** EV-0021…EV-0026.
**Unresolved:** No budget or measured cost profile was supplied.
