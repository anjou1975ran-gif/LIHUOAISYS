## Non-equivalences to pressure-test
- E1 vs E7: E1 changes a conceptual frame using internal negation; E7 detects *cross-method blind-spot relation* and generates a bridge candidate. If blinded reviewers cannot distinguish the traces, collapse or split the categories.
- E3 vs E6: E3 tests a hypothesis against a potential refuter; E6 adjudicates source/burden obligations across parties. One counterexample can serve both, but owners and exits differ.
- E4 vs E5: evidence update is not world intervention; live pilot needs authorization and rollback. Evidence may be generated by E5, then evaluated by E4.

## Missing families audit
- E8 ABDUCTIVE / BEST EXPLANATION: ADD_AS_RESEARCH_SLOT. Distinct explanatory candidate formation under underdetermined facts; demands comparison of rivals, discriminating evidence and no 'best' = truth.
- E9 DELIBERATIVE VALUE TRADEOFF: ADD_AS_RESEARCH_SLOT. Separate fact beliefs from normative criteria, cost-bearers, noncommensurability and who may decide; local exit is documented tradeoff or irreducible pluralism, not machine moral authorization.
- Causal / Counterfactual: OPEN SUBENGINE_OR_NEW_ENGINE TEST. Distinct intervention/counterfactual identification could merit a new engine only if E5 cannot express it without losing identification assumptions.
- Analogical / Case-Based: KEEP AS METHOD/HEURISTIC until demonstrated nontrivial update rule and failure-boundary distinctness; similarity can be evidence source, not inference authority.
- Constraint Satisfaction / Optimization: registered solver may belong to L9 when executable; the tradeoff/problem-formulation policy may belong to Method/Engine. Objective and constraints have human owners.
- Dialectical Materialism / other dialectical traditions: research as Method / theory lens first; independent Engine only with reproducible motion beyond a historical label.
