# Q1｜What remains when R1/R2/R3 and philosophical engines are removed?
**Council research verdict:** a governance contract remains; NOT a proven successor runtime.

## Minimal shell invariants — owner B architecture, bounded by C audit
1. Formation identity: a scoped claim/position and candidate basis must exist, with an accountable origin. Formation need not be a fixed numbered round.
2. Counterforce admissibility: any relevant challenge, contradiction, test, uncertainty or observation can enter; counterforce is not automatically a winner and cannot rewrite the owner's statement.
3. Material Delta: identifiable change to premise, evidence, object, scope, obligation, or inference must be source-linked; IMMATERIAL may preserve KEEP/QUIET.
4. Owner-preserving rejudgment: a qualified owner may KEEP / REVISE / WITHDRAW / BRANCH / HOLD; an opponent or router only triggers reconsideration.
5. Append-only history: original claim, challenger, basis, engine invocation, switch and response are preserved. SUPERSEDES != RESPONDS_TO != TRIGGERED_BY != CHALLENGES.
6. Qualification / authority separation: movement output != truth != action authority; scope, evidence, external authorization and failure states survive an exit.
7. Scoped HOLD / useful KEEP: lack of evidence or authority blocks only affected claim/action; silent non-change is valid.
8. Exit admission: engine-specific stop certificate is necessary when engine runs, but shell-level governance gate is separate and cannot be waived by an engine.
9. Engine-route accountability: every selected engine has a purpose and failure condition; unneeded engines may be skipped, with a qualified reason.
10. Observable provenance: preserve actual visible candidate/counterforce/update/trace once, SGER/receipt when current L8 requires; no invented private neural record.

## What is NOT invariant
- Specific R1/R2/R3 names or universal two-win criterion: implementation semantics of current L8, not a demonstrated timeless ontology.
- Synthesis: Socratic aporia or local falsification survival may be legitimate exits.
- Every problem must switch engines, every seat speaks every Council round, or every question must trigger L8.
- Router may decide the substantive winner; Registry label can confer truth.

## Two-layer exit
ENGINE_EXIT: qualified local state (e.g. APORIA, SURVIVE_FOR_NOW, WORLD_FEEDBACK_PENDING).
SHELL_EXIT: owner acceptance, material history, evidence standing, authorization, unresolved minority and current mandate's final gate.
ENGINE_EXIT != SHELL_RELEASE. Under V1.4.2 current mandatory L8 cycle still operates as written; this proposal does not bypass it.

## Falsifier
If no independent engine-specific counterforce/update/exit trace can be distinguished under controlled tests, the proposed separation is decorative and must be REVISE/REJECT rather than patching L8.
