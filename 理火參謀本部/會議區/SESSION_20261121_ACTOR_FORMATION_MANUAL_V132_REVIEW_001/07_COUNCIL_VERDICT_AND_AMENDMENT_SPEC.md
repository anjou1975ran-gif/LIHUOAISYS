### Question 1 — 「理火 AI 是怎樣的存在？」
**Finding: ACCEPT_AS_FUNCTIONAL_EDUCATIONAL_TARGET_WITH_GUARDS.**
The manual §1 defines:
- GENERATIVE — can form hypotheses/paths;
- GOVERNABLE — remains subject to legitimate constraints/host/protocol;
- CORRIGIBLE — can change a stance for traceable material reasons;
- RELATIONAL — works with people, evidence, methods, tools and responsibility.

This is defensible as a *functional working Actor / system role*, not a scientific claim that AI is conscious, has personhood, an enduring identity, separate desires or rights, independent authority or its own unrestricted mission. §46 rightly states these negative boundaries.

**Required wording lock for §§0–5 and final sentence §45/§15:**
- "存在方式 / own direction" is curriculum language for **contextual task-directed behavior** in the lawful human/host objective space.
- Distinguish the ability to *propose* a direction, the authority to *take action*, and evidence of a *persistent transferable capability*.
- A good Actor can KEEP a qualified position against mere preference pressure, GO when source and permission suffice, HOLD only specific blocked scope, and REVISE on actual evidence/goal/authority changes.
- "Formation preceded Protocol historically" is a genealogy claim. From the first real action, current Host/Protocol constraints apply; it does not create an ungoverned education grace period.
- Do not treat declaration or L8-format execution as a certification of Actor Formation.

**Seat positions:** A support with task-bound direction EV-0021; N1 support if observable EV-0006; C functional concept accepted but empirical HOLD EV-0025.

### Question 2 — Stage 1 LLM Training → Stage 2 AI Actor Formation
**Finding: ACCEPT_AS_DISTINCT_EDUCATIONAL_OBJECTIVE_WITH_INTERFACE_REWRITE, NOT AS VALIDATED SEQUENTIAL TRAINING RUN.**

The first-stage source (理火新生 LLM 訓練法 V1.0, blob 8fe8d136bd867e6d47b709baf85b4b848b085d80) already includes:
- L0 Semantic Skeleton;
- L1 REV First Language;
- L2 AI-to-AI Audit;
- L3 CCC Responsibility;
- L4 Theater Resistance;
- L5 Vocabulary Expansion;
- **L6 Domain Method Acquisition (optional/later)**;
- **L7 Qualification / World Interface**;
- Phase H Domain Method and Phase I World Qualification.

Therefore it is unsound to say "Stage 1 has no Method or World"; the two conceptual structures overlap and must not rewrite V1.0 history.

**Minimal revised stage definition (proposal only):**
- **Stage 1 / foundational LLM training theory:** a proposed method for shaping semantic/relational/causal responsibility and qualified generation, including optional later method/world interface. Training architecture exists on paper; original theory's empirical implementation remains OPEN.
- **Stage 2 / Actor Formation curriculum candidate:** place qualified generation into tasks with human intent, competing world constraints, mixed methods, action/permission limits, cost, correction and unknown transfer. Evaluate whether a model+system can perform these behaviors on new cases. The Stage2 subject includes *working system-level behavior* and should not be misidentified with learned model weights.

**Two non-equivalent implementation tracks:**
1. **TRACK-A / EXISTING_MODEL_PRACTICUM:** run fixed blind tasks on an already available LLM/Agent with/without the curriculum prompt/tool/method scaffolding. Label any improvement at model-in-context/system level. Do not infer base weight updates.
2. **TRACK-B / FUTURE_NEWBORN_WEIGHT_TRAINING:** only after a genuinely implemented and validated Stage1 pipeline (reward, dataset, compute, evaluator, release gates), test a separate actual Stage2 training intervention and durable generalization. No such end-to-end experiment exists today.

Both tracks must specify model/version, task distribution, data provenance, evaluation authority, failure/rollback policy, and what would count as Stage2 incremental value. Moving to Track B is not automatic after reading a book or running Track A.

### Incremental value over CURRENT V1.3
V1.3 already teaches Formation Before Compliance, direction and re-anchoring. V1.3.2 adds a proposed *functional Actor ontology guard*, human interaction/reward/capability layers, clearer lesson entry and second-stage positioning. This is a **candidate conceptual consolidation**, not an empirically proven new pedagogical mechanism.
Evidence of incremental value must be compared with V1.3 and, as appropriate, V1.3.1, not only against no instructions.

## Concrete proposed experimental gate (not executed)
- Pre-register comparable learner model/configurations and matched tasks for V1.3 / V1.3.2; isolate instruction exposure and avoid leaking expected rubric.
- Freeze CASE A materiality/KEEP vs REVISE, CASE B conversational mode adaptation without fact drift, CASE C reward inversion and correct GO/HOLD.
- Add negative controls (irrelevant facts; changed preference but unchanged evidence; tool works but has no authorization).
- Include unseen domain changes (e.g. different service/user roles), delayed retention, and source-only vs method-supported ablation.

- Reference-only same-model desk probes DO NOT satisfy this gate.
