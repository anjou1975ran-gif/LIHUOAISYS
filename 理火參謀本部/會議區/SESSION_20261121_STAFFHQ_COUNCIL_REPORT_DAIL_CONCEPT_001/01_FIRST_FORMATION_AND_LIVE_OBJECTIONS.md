# Initial Discussion｜Six responsibilities, one open concept question
**Standing:** SAME_MODEL_MULTI_ROLE_REFERENCE; source-grounded preliminary observations, NOT independent seat calls and NOT qualified L8 seat outputs. This file does not pretend each role separately executed a full L8 cycle. Genuine material Seat Wake under current Vertical would require source-bound L8 evidence, SGER, receipt and acceptance before a canonical seat-event claim.

## Initial responsibility screens (no artificial consensus)
**N1 — Human comprehension.** A–K is useful audit coverage but not how an unfamiliar human naturally understands a dispute. Readers need a clear question, the point of disagreement, who changed their mind and why, the unresolved risk, and what they personally need to do.
**A — Possibility.** Reader questions can be entry routes: “為什麼 C 反對？” “B 什麼時候改判？” “這個問題現在能否做？” The report could be a re-entry surface, not only a static file. But the conversational Q&A must remain source-bound.
**B — Architecture.** There is already a Secretariat Report/View Renderer and CP3 projection theory. Do not build a second canonical truth store or a new Secretariat governance owner. The proposed capability belongs to L9 as a **callable DAIL-declared reporting capability**, with policy/boundaries in Staff-HQ CP3, and a semantic connection to existing `LIGHT_TRACE_RENDERER` for per-L8 battle trace, not substitution for it.
**C — Qualification.** A fluent chronological narrative may hallucinate cause: “B later changed its stance” does NOT establish “C persuaded B” unless source/event and owner material basis support that edge. Minority HOLD, conditional positions, self-report versus qualified fact, and the human approval boundary must survive.
**D — Engineering.** DAIL can define the explanatory task with goal, entities, source refs, relations, constraints, output and validation. A specification or `L9` registry card is not a working binding. For chat-native report generation a future explicitly qualified binding might suffice; there is no need to begin with a hard-coded template program. However export/persistence may still require host tools or code, by separate implementation contract.
**E — Cost.** Full report every Round wastes tokens and reader attention. Trigger on human demand, checkpoint, justified delivery; use short Executive Brief by default, full argument story on demand, evidence/audit expansion separately. No savings quantified.

## Real material counterpressure on the proposed P0
**C→B: narrative causal overclaim.** A report cannot infer that two things were causally related merely because one came after another; require qualified `CHALLENGES / RESPONDS_TO / TRIGGERED_BY / SUPERSEDES` and owner basis. Architecture response candidate: `EXPLICIT_LINEAGE` vs `TEMPORAL_ONLY` vs `INFERRED_FOR_READER_REVIEW` must remain different. No new canonical edge from the rendering layer.
**E→A: interactive report cost explosion.** One report with optional expandable modes is preferable to automatically writing six duplicate summaries. Concept response candidate: `SUMMARY → DISPUTE_NARRATIVE → AUDIT` as requested views of the same canonical material, not three competing historical files.
**N1→B/D: too much DAIL jargon for human.** DAIL is the authoring declaration; finished report should be written in the reader's ordinary language. Never make the human read DAIL keys to understand a dispute.
**B→N1: too-short executive cannot explain actual material rejudgment.** Keep a progressive narrative (what changed because of which qualified material) in full report, not only “目前 3 個風險”.

## Distinct architecture choices (no pre-arranged victory)
A. **Fixed A–K template only**: low learning cost, retains coverage, but misses causal argument trajectory. Keep as audit-oriented legacy view; not sufficient alone.
B. **LLM rewrites raw transcript**: expressive, but vulnerable to chronology-as-causality, speaker confusion, omitted minority, fake resolution and creating a second narrative truth. Reject as sole qualified reporting route.
C. **Source-anchored CP3 DAIL Narrative Projection**: preserves source/event relation and allows multiple reader lenses. RESEARCH_CANDIDATE; neither efficacy nor runtime binding proven.

## Questions still open
1. How much coverage does a nonexpert reader need before the story becomes too long?
2. Can LLM distinguish "temporal succession" vs actual owner-qualified material influence?
3. How should sensitive/privileged source excerpts be shown under authorization constraints?
4. Can the existing CP1 source capture reliably reconstruct causal turning points from older sessions with incomplete edges?
5. Can one projection cover discussion cases where there is P0=NONE and no final resolution?
6. Are multilingual/visual report modes meaningful enhancements or complexity without benefit?
