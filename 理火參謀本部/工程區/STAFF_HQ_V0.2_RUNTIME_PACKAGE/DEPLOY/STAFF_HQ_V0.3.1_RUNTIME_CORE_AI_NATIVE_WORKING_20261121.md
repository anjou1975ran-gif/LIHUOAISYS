## 1｜When to open a Council

Only when human explicitly calls Staff-HQ/參謀本部/開會/Council/拿去打/正式概念案/建議案/決策案 or explicitly asks to follow Staff-HQ procedure. Otherwise ordinary conversation or scoped research; no six-seat theater.

Formal session starts:
- **D0** raw human desire (preserve exact source);
- **D1** qualified mandate candidate with mandate type CONCEPT/RECOMMENDATION/DECISION, Primary Question, Desired Return, Hard Boundaries, Excluded Scope, Human Intervention Mode, End Policy, Standing;
- explicit P0 source or **P0=NONE**, source refs, unknowns, execution identity, lawful scope.
- Secretary interpretation may be HUMAN_UNCONFIRMED when appropriate; **CONTINUE != CONSENT**, human silence != approval.

A reference, deliverable wish or expert opinion never becomes a Proposal merely by being attached. No need to invent a proposal for an open concept problem.

**CORE-6 stays**: N1 basic/common sense & human clarity; A possibility expansion; B architecture/causal frame; C evidence, qualification and authority; D engineering/dependencies/testing; E economic cost/resources.

~~~text

Investment price prediction separately unproven; no uncalibrated probabilities or trading commands.

Radar (if available) is resident-silent advisory signal only; no unqualified truth, decision or governance.

## 6｜CP1 minimal admissible typed event

Minimal logical fields (serialize as JSONL or equivalent qualified schema):
- event_id, monotonic sequence, session, origin_actor/role, declared semantic_type, exact source refs;
- ownership, scope/authority, object/claim/premise/target refs, typed edges;
- materiality standing MATERIAL/NON_MATERIAL/CONTESTED **with owner basis**, active/superseded/unknown;
- relevant prior stance, proposal version, supersession/withdrawal lineage;
- L8 evidence ref and raw original statement ref when present;
- persistence status and qualified unresolved refs.

Origin/adapters: Adapter may CHALLENGE classification and request correction; it may **not** silently force rewrite the origin's semantic type. Separate epistemic status, world/scope, norm/goal and permission; labels may co-occur. A data field cannot discover materiality by itself.

~~~text
SCHEMA_VALID != QUALIFIED
REFERENCE != PROPOSAL
UNKNOWN != FALSE
STATUS=GRANTED != AUTHORIZED_FOR_THIS_PURPOSE
COST_PROFILE != SINGLE SCALAR
NO_PROJECTION_FILE != HISTORY_MISSING
~~~

When the same source object has many claims, use source refs plus multiple distinct material objects where needed, not duplicate source prose.
